# BHĀRATA KALĀ - Indian Art Heritage Portal

An interactive, responsive digital heritage portal celebrating classical and indigenous Indian art, architecture, and contemporary modernism across millennia. Built with Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript with zero build dependencies.

### ⏳ Project 1: Interactive Timeline with Artifacts
* **Component:** CLA-I Assignment 1 • **Course Outcome:** CO1 • **Marks:** 10 Marks
* **Curatorial Overview:** A chronological digital museum tracing the stylistic, material, and philosophical evolution of Indian art across five monumental epochs (2500 BCE &ndash; 1760 CE).
* **14 Curated Canonical Artifacts:**
  1. *The Dancing Girl* (Mohenjo-Daro, Indus Valley &ndash; 2500 BCE)
  2. *Didarganj Yakshi* (Patna, Mauryan &ndash; 3rd C. BCE)
  3. *Lion Capital of Ashoka* (Sarnath &ndash; 250 BCE)
  4. *Sanchi Great Stupa & Toranas* (Madhya Pradesh &ndash; 1st C. BCE)
  5. *Ajanta Cave Frescoes* (Maharashtra &ndash; 5th C. CE)
  6. *Descent of the Ganges & Shore Temple* (Mamallapuram &ndash; 7th C. CE)
  7. *Kailasa Rock-Cut Temple* (Ellora Caves &ndash; 8th C. CE)
  8. *Kandariya Mahadeva Temple* (Khajuraho &ndash; 1030 CE)
  9. *Chola Nataraja Bronze* (Thanjavur &ndash; 11th C. CE)
  10. *Konark Sun Temple & Stone Chariot Wheel* (Odisha &ndash; 1250 CE)
  11. *Vijayanagara Stone Chariot & Vitthala Temple* (Hampi &ndash; 15th C. CE)
  12. *Tanjore Gold Foil Painting* (Thanjavur &ndash; 16th C. CE)
  13. *Jahangir Weighing Prince Khurram* (Mughal Court &ndash; 1615 CE)
  14. *Radha of Kishangarh (Bani Thani)* (Rajasthan &ndash; 1760 CE)
* **Features:** Era filter pills, interactive inspection modal with high-definition imagery, historical context, and material analysis.

### 🎨 Project 4: Cave Paintings Storyboard (Ajanta Cave Murals)
* **Component:** CLA-II Assignment 1 • **Course Outcome:** CO2 • **Marks:** 10 Marks • **Team Size:** 5
* **Narrative:** *The Mahajanaka Jataka* from Ajanta Cave 1 (Vakataka Dynasty, 5th Century CE).
* **Sequential Storyboard Panels:**
  1. *Panel 1 &ndash; The Prince's Dilemma:* Prince Mahajanaka's resolve to embark on the perilous sea voyage to Suvarnabhumi despite court pleas.
  2. *Panel 2 &ndash; The Oceanic Shipwreck:* The catastrophic ocean tempest, breaking ship timbers, and the prince's steadfast composure.
  3. *Panel 3 &ndash; Struggle for Survival:* Seven days and nights swimming through the dark abyss, adhering to spiritual perseverance (*Viriya-Paramita*).
  4. *Panel 4 &ndash; Divine Intervention:* The sea goddess Manimekhala descending to rescue the righteous prince and carry him to salvation.
* **Ajanta Technical Analysis:** Continuous narrative composition, tempera on dry mud-plaster (secco), and the classical mineral pigment palette (Lapis Lazuli, Red/Yellow Ochre, Terre Verte, Kaolin, Lamp Black).

### 🗿 Project 5: Sculpture Thematic Analysis (Mahabalipuram)
* **Component:** CLA-II Assignment 2 • **Course Outcome:** CO2 • **Marks:** 10 Marks
* **Format:** Academic Research Paper (.DOCX &bull; 7+ Pages) & Interactive Digital Gallery
* **Scholarly Focus:** Monolithic and rock-cut architectural typologies of the Pallava dynasty (7th&ndash;8th Century CE).
* **Core Chapters:**
  1. *Introduction & The Pallava Rock-Cut Landscape:* Cave temples (Mandapas), monolithic rathas, open-air bas-reliefs, and structural maritime shrines.
  2. *The Epic Bas-Relief (Descent of the Ganges / Arjuna's Penance):* Hydrological cleft, nagas, meditating ascetic, Shiva's Pasupata boon, satirical cat, and elephant family.
  3. *Architectural Evolution (Pancha Rathas):* Dharmaraja, Bhima, Arjuna, Draupadi, and Nakula-Sahadeva shrines.
  4. *Cave Sanctuaries & Dramatic Reliefs:* Mahishasuramardini Cave (Durga vs. Mahishasura) and Varaha lifting Bhudevi.
  5. *Synthesis of Natural and Divine:* Pastoral realism in Krishna Mandapa (cowherd milking cows, village flautists, agrarian harmony).
  6. *Political Legitimacy & Maritime Trade:* Royal portraits and diplomatic cultural beacons along the Indian Ocean silk routes.
  7. *Artistic, Religious & Cultural Legacy:* Influence on Chola, Vijayanagara, and Southeast Asian temple architecture.
* **Deliverable Available:** Direct download of the complete 3,600+ word academic submission research paper in `.docx` format (`Project 5 - Mahabalipuram Sculpture Thematic Analysis.docx`).

### 🖌️ Project 6: Regional Painting Fusion Project & Modern Heritage
* **Component:** CLA-I Assignment 3 • **Course Outcome:** CO2 / CO5 • **Marks:** 10 Marks • **Team Size:** 5
* **Part A &ndash; Regional Painting Fusion Masterpieces:**
  * *Fusion 1 (Warli & Kalamkari):* Minimalist tribal geometry blended with opulent botanical temple scroll borders.
  * *Fusion 2 (Madhubani & Pattachitra):* Mithila double-line vitality meets Odisha epic cloth scrollwork.
  * *Fusion 3 (Gond & Tanjore):* Tribal animist dotted wildlife textures harmonized with 22-karat gold foil relief.
* **Part B &ndash; Progressive Artists' Group (PAG) Modernist Heritage:**
  * *S. H. Raza's "Bindu":* Exploration of the cosmic seed, space, and meditative stillness through sacred geometry and primary color vibrations.
* **Deliverable Available:** High-resolution exhibition poster in vector PDF (`Project 6 - Regional Painting Fusion Poster.pdf`) with dedicated contributor credits at the bottom right, editable via Canva.
